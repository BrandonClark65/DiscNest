## DiscNest Discovery Plan (Marketplace-First, Community-Driven)

### Core belief

Disc golfers don’t want ads.  
They want better ways to buy, sell, and trade discs **without Facebook chaos**.

---

## 🧭 Primary Positioning

### What DiscNest Actually Is

DiscNest is a **disc-golf-native used marketplace** that happens to also have a strong bag builder.

### What It Replaces

- Facebook group chaos  
- Comment-claim selling  
- DMs with no structure  
- Lost discs, ghosting, no context  
- “PM me” with zero transparency  

### How to Talk About It (Internally & Externally)

- **Never say**
  - “It’s a bag builder with a marketplace”

- **Always say**
  - “I built a cleaner way for disc golfers to buy/sell/trade discs — bags and stats just help give context.”

---

## 🎯 Target Users (Marketplace First)

### Primary

- Players who buy used discs  
- Players who flip backups  
- Players who cycle molds  
- Players tired of Facebook selling  

### Secondary

- Bag tinkerers  
- Data-driven throwers  

---

## Phase 1: Ground Truth & Language Testing (Days 1–7)

### 🎯 Goal

Learn how players already complain about disc selling — and mirror that language.

### 🔍 Reddit: Listening Before Posting

- Spend 3–5 days commenting on posts like:
  - “Is this disc worth anything?”
  - “Where do you sell discs now?”
  - “Why is Facebook selling so bad?”

- Reply value-first, **no links**:
  - “Yeah FB selling works, but the lack of structure is brutal.”  
  - “Half the time I just want to see condition + plastic + price in one place.”

This primes the audience before DiscNest ever appears.

---

## Phase 2: Reddit Value Posts (Days 7–30)

### Rule

You only drop a link if someone asks — or in a **comment reply**, never the OP.

### 🟠 Exact Reddit Post Templates (Copy/Paste)

#### 🥏 Post Template 1: Selling Pain Point (High Performer)

- **Title**
  - *What’s the most annoying part of selling discs right now?*

- **Body**
  - Genuine question.  
  - Is it:
    - Facebook comment-claim chaos  
    - DMs with no follow-through  
    - No standard for condition  
    - Hard to price fairly  
    - Having to explain every disc 10 times  
  - Curious what actually frustrates people the most.

- **Follow-up comment (if engagement starts)**
  - “This is super helpful — seems like structure + transparency is the big gap.”
  - (Link only if asked.)

---

#### 🥏 Post Template 2: Marketplace Comparison (Subtle)

- **Title**
  - *Where do you actually prefer buying used discs?*

- **Body**
  - Facebook groups?  
  - Local shop bins?  
  - Friends?  
  - Reddit?  
  - Something else?  
  - Not asking what’s most popular — asking what feels the least painful.

- **If someone says “none” or complains**
  - “Yeah, that matches my experience. Selling/buying feels way harder than it should be.”

---

#### 🥏 Post Template 3: Disc Pricing Reality Check

- **Title**
  - *How do you decide what a used disc is worth?*

- **Body**
  - Condition?  
  - Plastic?  
  - Run?  
  - What you paid?  
  - Vibes?  
  - I always feel like pricing is half guesswork. Curious how others approach it.

This tees up DiscNest’s structured listings naturally.

---

#### 🥏 Post Template 4: Trade-Focused (Underserved)

- **Title**
  - *Does anyone actually like trading discs anymore?*

- **Body**
  - Trades sound great in theory, but in practice I’ve found them messy.  
  - Curious if people still trade a lot, or if most have moved to buy/sell instead.

---

#### 🥏 Post Template 5: Soft Reveal (After Engagement)

- **Title**
  - *I got tired of selling discs through Facebook — so I tried building something cleaner*

- **Body**
  - Not a promo post — genuinely looking for feedback.  
  - I was frustrated with:
    - comment-claim selling  
    - zero structure  
    - repeating the same info over and over  
  - So I’ve been building a disc-golf-native marketplace where:
    - condition/plastic are structured  
    - listings stay organized  
    - bags give context to sellers  
  - If anyone wants to poke holes in it, I’d love that.

- **Linking**
  - 👉 Link at bottom **only after 3–5 comments exist.**

## 🧠 Feedback Questions to Ask Users

When someone tries DiscNest, ask:

- What felt better than Facebook?  
- What felt worse?  
- What did you expect that wasn’t there?  
- Would you list another disc — why or why not?  

These answers guide feature prioritization and future messaging.

---

## 🧯 Anti-Spam Guardrails

- ✔ Never link in first post  
- ✔ Never say “just launched”  
- ✔ Never argue with criticism  
- ✔ Always thank skeptics  
- ✔ Ask for feedback more than users  

---

## 📈 Success Metrics (Ignore Traffic)

Watch for:

- People saying “This is cleaner than FB”  
- Feature requests  
- Repeat sellers  
- Organic shares  
- “I wish this existed earlier” comments
